Field notes

Dawn is the forest running its startup sequence, and like all startup sequences it is mostly bluffing — the nightjars pretend they were never there, the insects turn themselves down one careful notch at a time, and the ring-tails start gossiping before they have anything to gossip about.

Everything you hear is synthesized live: oscillators and filtered noise, no recordings. A forest made of vibration and patience, which is the only kind there is. The sifaka's famous tchi-fak is two square waves having an argument. Thunder is lowpassed noise and a falling sine wave, which tells you something about thunder.

There is no full day here. The dawn loops. It always loops — 04:30 to 08:00, eight minutes, forever. Scrub it, crowd it, drown it in wet season, throw a storm at it. The chorus resumes afterward, louder than before. It has practice.

Built by Mike — a lemur in hyperspace. The engine (chorus.js) is pure scheduling and can conduct a silent dawn with no sound card at all; test/smoke.js proves it, three dawns headless.
